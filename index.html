01 / ABOUT THE HACKATHON

A shared space for
practical discovery.

How can AI help us understand what’s happening inside operational technology systems?

2026 OT Hackathon is a collaborative research hackathon exploring how artificial intelligence and data-driven techniques can support operational technology security. Researchers and cybersecurity professionals will investigate telemetry, detect unusual behavior, and develop intelligent investigation approaches.

The format centers on hands-on experimentation: exploring data, connecting tools, and developing research prototypes through academic–government collaboration.
